What is Cortex City?

Cortex City is a state-of-the-art spatial-temporal AI traffic digital twin designed for modern urban environments. It mirrors a city's road grid, analyzing real-time vehicle flow, weather patterns, and local constraints to simulate and predict congestion citywide.

An Urban Emergency Chronicle

The Emergency

At 6:30 PM, heavy monsoon rains descend upon Mangaluru, instantly flooding the arterial highway link near Kuntikan Junction. Standard navigation apps show a clear green path, unaware of the sudden flash flood build-up. Stuck in this growing gridlock is an emergency ambulance transporting a critical patient. The vehicle is trapped behind hundreds of cars, with travel speeds reduced to a complete halt. Standard routing systems react only after traffic has accumulated, providing zero forward warning to emergency dispatchers. Because of this predictive blindness, the ambulance loses twenty crucial minutes in gridlock. This delay highlights the fatal limitation of today's reactive navigation: it cannot anticipate weather-induced bottlenecks. Cortex City was built to solve this exact nightmare. By combining spatial road models with real-time weather coefficients, it predicts bottlenecks twenty minutes before they form, guiding emergency vehicles and citizens to safety.

System Blueprints

Project Architecture & Training

Cortex City runs on a high-availability infrastructure stack utilizing state-of-the-art spatial-temporal AI models.

Ubuntu Linux OS Environment
Deployed on Ubuntu Server OS with Docker containerization, hosting Python-based HTTP telemetry APIs and OSRM microservices.
Overpass & Open-Meteo APIs
Extracts dynamic city street vectors from OpenStreetMap and live wind/rain statistics from weather engines.
AI MODEL ARCHITECTURE
Spatial GNN (Graph Neural Network) coupled with temporal LSTM layers to forecast propagation of congestion waves across nodes.
MODEL TRAINING METHOD
Trained in PyTorch using 3 months of historical traffic congestion densities from Google Maps Traffic, establishing weather-decay weight regressions.
CLIENT INFERENCE ENGINE
Compiled weights are compiled into high-performance JS matrix evaluations, executing sub-millisecond local speed adjustments as time slider changes.
